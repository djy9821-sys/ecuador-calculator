Method, assumptions and sources

Main calculation

Electricity per day = power × average load × running hours. Electricity per cycle = electricity per day × days per cycle. Cost = electricity × tariff. Cost per year = cost per cycle × cycles per year. The average load is the mean of four stages, each one quarter of the cycle. Both aerator types are compared at the same number of units.

Aerator data

New aerator: SC15 with four wheels, SOTR 2.64 kg O₂/h and 1.64 kW measured input (test report JS2024TJ03119J, Jiangsu Provincial Agricultural Machinery Test and Appraisal Station, December 2024). Old aerator: 1.5 kW (2 HP) and 1.4 kg O₂/kWh are assumptions until they are measured.

Load pattern

The new aerator is assumed to run at 40, 50, 70 and 90 % of full power over the four stages. For the sample pond these values are above the smallest sufficient load in every stage of the oxygen check. The real speed depends on pond conditions and on the farmer. Boyd & McNevin (2021) describe aerators running at full capacity during the first part of grow-out on many farms.

Oxygen check

Shrimp respiration: R = a · Wb from Vinatea et al. (2011), Table 1. Water-column and sediment respiration: 0.2 / 1.0 / 2.0 mg O₂/L/h, tied to standing biomass of 2 / 8 / 24 t/ha. This is usually the largest part of the night demand and the one most worth measuring. Water exchange is credited evenly over 24 hours. Field transfer: OTR = SOTR × (Cs − Cmin) / 9.09 × 1.024(T−20) × α, with saturation from Weiss (1970). Photosynthesis and natural re-aeration are not counted, which is conservative. Oxygen output is taken as proportional to power, which is also conservative.

Tariff and exchange rates

PLN tariffs for October to December 2026: B-2 Rp 1,444.70 and B-3 / I-3 Rp 1,114.74 per kWh, before tax and other bill items (Metro TV News, 29 October 2026). Exchange rates are rounded market rates of 3 November 2026 and can be edited.
